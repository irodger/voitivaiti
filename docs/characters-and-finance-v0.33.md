## Household events

Finance state lives on Character.home so it follows the person across company changes. Initialization of old saves starts a fresh cycle without charging past months. Initial random encounter after 10–17 days, later intervals 14–28 days. Deterministic seed/character/day selection prevents rerolling on reload. Inheritance can happen once per character. Repairs retain owned items because the billed repair restores them. A monitor bill requires an owned home monitor; otherwise the small repair concerns a phone.

Event amounts are authored game prices, not claims about actual rent, healthcare or repairs. Leak -5000, small raffle +500/1000, refund +800, inheritance +20000, monitor/phone -2500, bed -1800, clinic -3500. The injury is a narrated expense, not a new medical or sick-leave subsystem.

Existing daily living costs now deduct 600 less; rent 15000 and transit 3000 are paid every 30 calendar days. At the baseline, 600 daily plus 18000 monthly preserves the previous average 1200/day. Monthly and random bills retain the unpaid amount instead of disappearing at zero cash. Outstanding bills are paid from later income at daily settlement. A bounded ledger keeps 40 entries; Home shows the latest five and remaining balance. Random income is separated from salary reporting but included in routine/vacation totals.
