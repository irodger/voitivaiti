1. The player sees forecast and previous capacity, then requests the exact owner through the saved dependency. The owning team contact is created on that request, not at company generation.

3. Existing scope and verification actions choose bounded work or a proper result. Coverage is saved only when the action outcome is committed: 2x bounded, 3x verified, or evidence without a claimed production change.
4. A bounded result's existing delayed consequence returns the same problem with the uncovered traffic boundary, previous task/decision, source department, owner and consumer. A later proper fix supersedes that open boundary and causes Product review. The Product contact is created only when that review is actually assigned.

The existing scene graphs, numerical effects, outcome delays, queue and causal consumption are retained. This adds no mail, dismissal, reorganizations or industries.
