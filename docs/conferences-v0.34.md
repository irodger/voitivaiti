# v0.34.0 — conferences and office movement

Senior and higher grades can choose a professional conference session from an office invitation. Online attendance takes 90 game minutes and is free; visiting takes 180 minutes and costs 4,000 game rubles including travel. These are authored game prices.

The two sessions cover checking conflicting evidence and handing work over with context. Their saved notes are accessible from the office. Attendance does not automatically change project health, mastery or promotion. Existing tasks stay open.

Invitation decisions persist on the character, including across reloads and company changes. Invitations return after 45 calendar days. Pending meetings, incidents, insufficient time and insufficient cash have explicit explanations. Skipping is always available. Expenses appear in the personal finance ledger and cannot be charged twice.

Office colleagues now have five different short-walk destinations rather than sharing one endpoint. No new artwork or collision engine was introduced.

Validation: conference regression tests cover grade eligibility, cooldown, reload, payment idempotence, unchanged production state, free participation and blocked conditions. Full suite and production build run for this release.
