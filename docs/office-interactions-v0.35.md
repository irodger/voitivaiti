# v0.35.0 — office interactions

Regular pending morning syncs now gather the team, as onboarding meetings already did. Review and QA bring the player and one relevant colleague to two discussion positions; the other colleagues stay at their desks. QA prefers a quality colleague, review a colleague in the same profession, with a roster fallback.

Hover or keyboard focus reveals the character's role, current activity and conversation availability. Names remain compact. Touch opens the existing conversation directly. Introductory labels remain inert. Ground shadows and press feedback improve readability without adding image assets.

Movement remains presentation of game state, not a collision simulation. No new economy or career rules.
